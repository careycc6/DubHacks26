Cart Court · getting started

Welcome to the court ⚖️

Five quick steps and your family can start texting the court. You can come back to this page any time.
1

Who's in the family

Add everyone's name, role (parent, teen, grandparent…), the phone they use now, and the number they text from. Battery health is optional.

Add your family →
2

Phone budget optional

How much the family can spend on phones over the whole plan, now and later. The court trims picks to fit and says what's left.

3

Connect iMessage

Cart Court lives in iMessage: each person texts the court's number, and it keeps everyone posted. This connects the court's number (via Photon).

Connect texting →
4

Say hello to everyone

The court texts each person a one-line welcome so they have its number saved. Nobody needs to install anything.

5

Add a co-parent optional

Let another parent sign in with their Google or Apple email. Only parents can clear the cart, set the budget or fix who a purchase was for.

Then try it from your phone

  • Text the court's number:
  • court, what phone should I get? for picks that fit you
  • Paste a store link to put a phone in the family cart
  • court, rule when the family is ready: BUY, WAIT or SKIP, with the savings
