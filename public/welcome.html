2

Connect iMessage with Photon, about 5 minutes

Cart Court lives in iMessage. Photon gives the court a real iMessage number: everyone texts it links and questions about anything they want to buy (phones, iPads, Macs, speakers, TVs…), and it texts back rulings. Nobody installs an app.

  1. Make a Photon project

    Sign up at app.photon.codes (free), create a project, and open its settings. Copy the Project ID and Project Secret.

  2. Give the court its keys

    Paste them here. They're saved to .env on this computer only.

    This court is hosted, so keys go in the host's settings, not on this page. In Vercel: Settings → Environment Variables, add these two, then Redeploy:

    PHOTON_PROJECT_ID   PHOTON_PROJECT_SECRET

  3. Point Photon at this court

    Hosted courts get messages by webhook. On the laptop with the project's .env, in the cart-court folder, run:

    It prints SPECTRUM_WEBHOOK_SECRET=… once. Add that in Vercel (plus PUBLIC_URL = this site's address) and Redeploy. Then stop any laptop copy of the court, or every text gets two replies.

  4. Add your family's numbers in Photon

      Not sure which number an iPhone sends iMessages from (some use an email)? Open debug.photon.codes on that phone.

    3

    Say hello to everyone

    The court texts each person a one-line welcome so they have its number saved. Nobody needs to install anything.

    4

    Family budget optional

    How much the family can spend on tech over the whole plan: phones, iPads, Macs, laptops, TVs, speakers, headphones. The court trims picks to fit, holds nice-to-haves first, and says what's left.

    5

    Add a co-parent optional

    Let another parent sign in with their Google or Apple email. Only parents can clear the cart, set the budget or fix who a purchase was for.

    Then try it from your phone

    • Text the court's number:
    • court, what phone should I get? or court, what laptop should I get? for picks that fit you
    • Paste any store link (Apple, T-Mobile, Best Buy, Amazon…) to put it in the family cart: phones, iPads, Macs, speakers, anything
    • court, rule when the family is ready: BUY, WAIT or SKIP, with the savings
