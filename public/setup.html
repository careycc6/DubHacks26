Cart Court · setup

Swear in the court

Step 1 is all you need to try it. Steps 2–3 put it on your family's phones.
← Dashboard

1 Gemini (the court's voice) checking…

Sign in with your Google account at aistudio.google.com/apikey, click Create API key, and paste it here. No key? The court still works in rules mode: the verdicts, prices and demo buttons need no AI.

2 Photon (a real iMessage number) optional checking…

At app.photon.codes, create a project and copy its Project ID and Project Secret.

3 Your household needs step 2 to text

The grey rows are an example family. Type over a row to make it a real person; rows you leave blank stay examples and are never saved or texted. Then Text everyone texts them all at once ("Welcome to the court, [name]! ⚖️"). Press it again after adding someone and only the new person gets texted. Photon's free plan gives each person their own 1:1 chat with the court (group chats need a paid dedicated line), and every chat shares the same family cart.

Battery health % is how much charge the battery still holds compared to new (not today's charge). Find it on iPhone: Settings → Battery → Battery Health (Maximum Capacity) · Pixel: Settings → Battery → Battery health · Galaxy: Samsung Members app → Phone diagnostics → Battery status. Don't know it? Leave it blank: the welcome text asks, and people can reply "battery health 85%".

Names and numbers are saved only on this computer (data/household.local.json, gitignored). Phone stats, prices and promos stay synthetic. Not sure which number an iPhone sends iMessages from? Open debug.photon.codes on that phone.

Keys are saved only to .env on this computer (it's in .gitignore). This page only works on localhost.
